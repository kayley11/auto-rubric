**Post-necrotising pancreatitis distal narrowing.** Prior pancreatic head necrosis and instrumentation can cause inflammatory stricturing, contributing to ductal dilatation and stone retention. No imaging in the record directly demonstrates a stricture, so this remains unconfirmed.
