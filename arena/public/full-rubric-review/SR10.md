Acanthosis nigricans is classically associated with insulin resistance, but this association is not specific. In this case, normal glucose argues against a primary metabolic driver, and the constellation of congenital skin laxity, sparse curly hair, enamel erosion, low bone mineral density, and cognitive impairment points toward a broader connective-tissue or developmental syndrome rather than an isolated metabolic process. Endocrine screening beyond glucose (insulin, IGF-1, thyroid function) and a formal developmental assessment are reasonable. Chest imaging and treatment response data are not yet available.
