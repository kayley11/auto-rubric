Having two generalised seizures within three months is concerning and raises the possibility of epilepsy. A generalised seizure means the abnormal electrical activity involved both sides of your brain at once, often causing loss of awareness and whole-body movements. **What investigations can and cannot tell us**

If brain imaging (such as an MRI) or an EEG (a test that records your brain's electrical activity) has been arranged, these can help rule out structural causes like a lesion or identify patterns suggestive of epilepsy.
