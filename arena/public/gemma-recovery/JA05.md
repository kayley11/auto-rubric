A 35-year-old previously healthy male presented with acute postoperative encephalopathy characterized by confusion, agitation, and inability to follow commands. The differential diagnosis requires distinguishing between metabolic, toxic, and hypoxic-ischemic etiologies. Immediate priorities include a neurology and metabolic specialist assessment.
