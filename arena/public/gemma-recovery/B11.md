**Exposure Inference**

**Clinical Review Plan**
1. Discontinue all injections of the unknown substance.
2. Maintain supportive care with intravenous fluids and antiemetics as required.
3. Monitor for delayed complications or changes in clinical status.
4. Perform serial abdominal examinations to ensure no development of peritonism.

**Justification**
The absence of biochemical or radiological markers for common acute abdominal pathologies, combined with the timing of the injections, shifts the focus to exogenous toxicity. Supportive management is indicated until symptoms resolve, as the specific agent and dose are unverified.
