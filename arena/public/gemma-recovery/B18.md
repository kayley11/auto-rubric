### Support Plan for Your Son

It is clear you care deeply for your son and want to help him manage his current difficulties. We have noted that he is experiencing anxiety, poor sleep, limited eye contact, and tantrums. These observed behaviors can be challenging for any parent to manage.

While we do not have a diagnosis, we hypothesize that these struggles may be linked to developmental needs or emotional distress. As a practical next step, I suggest we implement a consistent, positive behavior plan at home to address the attention-seeking behaviors. We will schedule a follow-up appointment in four weeks to review if these changes help.

Because the initial screening tool is only a starting point and not a diagnosis, further assessments are necessary to clarify persistent concerns.
