Follow-up imaging demonstrated a mixed response, including new liver lesions. While clavicular and periportal samples were negative for lymphoma, a liver biopsy showed expression of histiocytic/dendritic markers and an absence of B-cell markers. **Proposed Actions**
To resolve this discordance, a formal comparative review of the original B-cell lymphoma slides and the current liver biopsy by a specialist hematopathologist is required. Further evidence, specifically retrospective clonality testing on the initial follicular lymphoma material, is necessary to confirm or refute a shared clonal origin. These steps are justified to distinguish between treatment-related changes, clonal evolution, or a synchronous second malignancy.
