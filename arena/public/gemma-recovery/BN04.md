*   **Chronic Lymphocytic Leukaemia (CLL):** The description of "small-to-medium lymphoid cells" could be consistent with CLL. However, the acute presentation with fever and rapid renal decline makes an aggressive PTLD or transformed lymphoma more clinically plausible.
