Symptoms began approximately ten days prior with diarrhoea and myalgia. A prior creatinine of 456 µmol/L (2 August) shows a 351 µmol/L (77%) rise over seven days. 3. Drug-related contribution (ceftazidime, LMWH): both were administered after symptom onset, making them less likely as primary aetiology but possible contributors to renal injury.

**Next Actions**

Obtain peripheral smear (schistocytes), serum C3/C4, ANCA panel, anti-GBM antibody, haptoglobin, reticulocyte count, and stool Shiga toxin. Urine microscopy for casts is pending.
