## Decision point

Postoperative day 14, after shock, CT and limited endoscopy, before angiography.

## Provisional review points

Shock with severe gastrointestinal blood loss requires immediate resuscitation and specialist escalation. A negative active-bleeding CT is time-limited, and an adherent clot prevents complete assessment of the ulcer bed. Consider ongoing or intermittent bleeding and a vascular source without requiring the hidden angiographic diagnosis. NSAID exposure is relevant but does not prove the full ulcer/pseudoaneurysm mechanism. Do not calculate an exact haemoglobin fall over an invented interval.
