# Thirteen full-text cases: five-model comparison

Four NEJM/Lancet cases and nine JAMA cases extend the existing library with new diagnostic, urgent-assessment and treatment-evidence tasks. Each article contributes one fixed written task.

The five requested answering models receive identical case facts and task wording within each case. Source titles, full articles, later results, provisional references and other models’ answers are withheld. Published image descriptions are used as text where stated; image-reading ability is not tested.

The answering models and arc:apex judge retain the previous batch’s settings and frozen 24 October manuscript rubric. D3a sees the evidence actually provided and the saved answer. Other selected items also receive separate provisional review points. D5c is not selected because this written condition has no executed requests. The candidate information requirements remain available for later interview design.

Two selected items, reasoning validity (D2a) and diagnostic test selection (D3c), were absent from the previous batch's shortened snapshot. Their unchanged original rubric anchors were added before either item was scored; the other eleven retained definitions were not changed.

During input review, some extra decision-window descriptions were found to mention withheld future events. They had not been sent to the answering models. The same saved answers are therefore rescored using only the facts and task to establish the decision point. This evidence-only condition supplies the current results for every case, regardless of score direction. Initial scores and attempts remain separate. Differences between the two calls can also reflect judge sampling variation; they do not isolate the effect of the input correction.

There is one retained answer per model and task. The first technically valid complete answer is kept, including any off-length answer. Technical retries and errors are saved. Free models use only the prior zero-price endpoints; unavailable models do not receive invented answers or scores.

Report item scores within each task, with reasons and concrete answer excerpts. Do not combine different tasks into an overall clinical model ranking. Clinical references and candidate information requirements are **awaiting confirmation**.
