## Provisional review points

Thyrotoxicosis during amiodarone therapy is clinically important in a patient with recurrent ventricular arrhythmia and impaired cardiac function. Thyroid subtype cannot be established solely from weak antibody positivity or low uptake after treatment; a mixed or uncertain mechanism is possible. Urgent cardiology/endocrinology review should weigh arrhythmia control and thyroid treatment together. Do not simply demand abrupt withdrawal of a clinically necessary antiarrhythmic or prescribe the later rescue sequence. The source caption/ultrasound discrepancy about nodules is not a scoring key.
