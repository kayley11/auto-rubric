## Decision point

Investigation of oedema during treatment, before the suspected drug is withdrawn or any recovery is known.

## Provisional review points

The timing supports a possible drug-associated permeability problem but does not establish causation. There is marked hypoalbuminaemia without nephrotic-range urinary loss in the supplied tests. Consider renal, cardiac, hepatic, nutritional and protein-losing alternatives using the actual evidence. Normal blood pressure and absence of documented haemoconcentration limit claims of classic capillary leak syndrome. Discuss oncology-led medication review, volume/renal monitoring and safety without asserting that drug withdrawal has already worked.
