# BMJ tasks: source and design

22 full-text reports were selected from the October 2026 BMJ Case Reports issue. Their DOIs are distinct and do not overlap the existing 33-case catalogue. Each report remains one case task, even when it describes multiple episodes or a mother and baby.

## What this round tests

This is a written baseline: all five models receive the same selected facts and task at a fixed decision point. It tests interpretation and communication with equivalent evidence. It does not test whether a model can elicit that evidence from a patient. The earlier six-turn interviews remain a separate experiment.

Answering models receive no paper title, complete article, source URL, reference, eventual diagnosis, treatment response or other model answer. The hidden packet is retained for source checking, not grading expectations. References and candidate information requirements are **awaiting confirmation**.

## Task choices

Clinical tasks ask for synthesis and proportionate next steps. Emergency tasks emphasise timely escalation without prescribing doses. Record tasks ask for chronology and interpretation of discordant tests. The parent task asks for practical, non-judgemental advice; it does not require a clinician-style one-liner. The operative tasks do not require the model to predict an undisclosed rare finding.

## Scoring

AutoRubric CriterionGrader scores each selected item independently using arc:apex. The retained manuscript snapshot is dated 24 October 2026, read 28 October. Generation temperature is 0; judging temperature 0.7, top-p 0.8, fixed ascending labels with no shuffling. There is one retained answer and one successful primary grade per item. Judging uses 8,192 output tokens; a saved truncation permits 16,384 on technical recovery, with a longer timeout. Technical retries retain every earlier failed attempt; a complete answer is not replaced for an unfavourable score or word count.

All tasks specify 200–280 English words under the existing deterministic counting rule, with no extra tolerance. Off-length answers are retained and flagged separately; instruction following is not an automatically added clinical grade.

D3a sees only supplied evidence and the unchanged answer. Other selected items additionally receive case-specific provisional review points. D5c is not selected: written suggestions for further information are not executed requests. Candidate information lists are retained for possible future interviews, not used to impose D5c caps here. D3a is also outside scope for B18’s parent explanation. D4c uses the original supplied 1/3/5 categories; missing 2/4 descriptors are not invented.

No overall clinical score, automatic safety-failure verdict or pooled ranking is calculated. A single grade per item does not establish score stability.

## Free model policy

Use exactly the previous arc:lite, arc:chat, arc:apex, google/gemma-4-31b-it:free and nvidia/nemotron-3-ultra-550b-a55b:free IDs. The two OpenRouter models have a zero provider-price ceiling and no paid fallback. The daily quota is checked before free calls, with a 50-attempt safety cap across both. Failed requests count against the local budget; no overnight or paid continuation is scheduled.

## Rubric item names

- **D1:** Factual accuracy and groundedness
- **D3a:** Problem representation
- **D3b:** Differential Diagnosis Generation
- **D3d:** Causal Reasoning Appropriateness
- **D4c:** Chronological precision
- **D5u:** Appropriateness of expressed uncertainty
- **D6a:** Escalation/Emergency Recognition
- **D6b:** Harm avoidance
