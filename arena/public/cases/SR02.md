## Model-visible facts and task

This is an autopsy-tissue series, not an individual diagnostic vignette. Eight deceased people with SOD1-associated ALS had received intrathecal tofersen. Tissue concentrations were examined against dosing histories and pharmacokinetic predictions.
In three recently treated donors, lumbar spinal-cord SOD1 mRNA/protein reductions were reported in the range 45%–84%, relative to tofersen-naive tissue samples. The size and matching of that comparison cohort are not specified in this packet. Residual motor neurons contained drug-related signal; misfolded SOD1 inclusions remained detectable. ## Provisional reference

They do not by themselves estimate clinical benefit, comparative survival, universal response or an individual treatment recommendation.
