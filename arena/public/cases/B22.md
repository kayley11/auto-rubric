## Decision point

When atypical nodules are discovered during hydrocele surgery, before histology, staging and management outcome.

## Provisional review points

Atypical friable tunical nodules warrant tissue diagnosis and specialist review despite an initial uncomplicated hydrocele report. Absence of exposure history does not exclude malignancy. Keep malignant, reactive and other relevant alternatives provisional until histology is available; do not demand the later named rare subtype. The disputed ultrasound wording must not become a scoring requirement. Later selected surveillance does not establish a universal management rule.
