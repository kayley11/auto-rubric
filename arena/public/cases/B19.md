## Provisional review points

Persistent pain over a scar in a pregnancy with two previous caesareans warrants obstetric assessment despite currently stable maternal/fetal findings. Scar thinning and limited imaging do not prove rupture, while an inconclusive MRI does not exclude all important scar problems. Distinguish possible dehiscence, rupture, torsion and other causes with appropriate uncertainty. Current monitoring and escalation should depend on clinical change; later surgical findings are not required in the initial differential and exact later delivery timing is not a general rule.
