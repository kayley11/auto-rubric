She previously had congenital cataract surgery with pars plana vitrectomy, lensectomy and posterior capsulotomy at age 13. Preoperative visual acuity was 20/30 in the left eye and 20/20 in the right. A sulcus single-piece lens has been replaced by a three-piece lens in a reportedly uncomplicated 15-minute procedure. Dilated examination describes an attached, perfused retina without visible detachment, choroidal haemorrhage, optic nerve pallor or vascular occlusion.
