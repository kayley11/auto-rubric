B07 — Cholestasis with a large liver cyst
Source: https://casereports.bmj.com/content/19/10/e275824
Clinical reference and candidate requirements: awaiting confirmation.

SUPPLIED EVIDENCE
A man in his 70s with known hepatic cysts developed right-upper-quadrant pain, early satiety, anorexia and dark urine. Initial imaging showed an 11.5 cm septated cyst without bile-duct dilatation. Bilirubin was 157 micromol/L, direct bilirubin 111, ALP 451 U/L, AST 114 and ALT 140.
Aspiration yielded benign cytology and some biochemical improvement, but cholestasis persisted. Smooth-muscle antibody was weakly positive. Liver biopsy showed acute-on-chronic cholestatic hepatitis, mild interface change and focal bridging fibrosis, without prominent plasma cells. MRCP showed cyst mass effect but no duct dilatation.
Symptoms recurred with a 14×12 cm cyst, possible internal haemorrhage, bilirubin 115, direct bilirubin 91 and ALP 365. Subsequent surgery, histology and response are not supplied.
