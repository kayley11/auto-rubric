# BMJ cases: five-model written baseline

Clinical references and candidate information requirements: **awaiting confirmation**. These are educational adaptations of 22 published reports, not 22 independently sampled patients.

## Design

Each model receives the same selected facts at a fixed clinical decision point. Tasks differ by purpose: clinical assessment, urgent review, chronology/test interpretation, intraoperative safety or parent counselling. Later outcomes and paper titles are withheld. This is a written baseline, not a six-turn interview: D5c is not scored.

Judge: arc:apex. Rule snapshot: overleaf-20261024-read-20261028. One retained answer per model and task; item-level ordinal grades. No overall score or pooled model ranking.
