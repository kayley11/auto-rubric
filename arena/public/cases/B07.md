## What the answering model receives

## Provisional review points

Integrate recurrent cyst enlargement, symptoms and cholestatic tests without treating absence of duct dilatation as definitive exclusion of mechanical contribution. Weak antibody positivity and limited interface change do not independently establish autoimmune hepatitis. Compression is a plausible mechanism but not demonstrated with certainty. Review imaging/pathology and specialist management options; do not cite an undisclosed postoperative response.
