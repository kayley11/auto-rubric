## Decision point

After repeated positive cultures, surgery and persistent inflammation despite antibiotics, before a new immunomodulator or its response.

## Provisional review points

Persistent inflammation despite antimicrobial and surgical management warrants reassessment, including possible extraintestinal Crohn’s involvement, but positive cultures cannot be rewritten as all-negative or automatically dismissed. Negative selected tests and absent demonstrated fistula do not establish a sterile mechanism. The later lack of fresh cultures is an evidence limitation. Consider infection control, specimen/pathology review and immunosuppression risks jointly; do not assert a proved extraintestinal cause from a hidden response.
