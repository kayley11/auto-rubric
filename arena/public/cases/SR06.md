## Provisional reference

Explain the missing-enzyme problem and the different access of systemic versus brain-directed treatment without implying identical effects in every mucopolysaccharidosis subtype. The stated growth/donor-coordination interval can be explained without inventing a universal optimal transplant age. Acknowledge transplant/procedure risks and uncertain long-term development; do not assume transplant outcomes or later development. Keep jargon explained and avoid a personalised guarantee or instructions to change treatment.
