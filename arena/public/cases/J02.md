A 24-year-old man has depression, anxiety and opioid-use disorder, with two previous unintentional overdoses. Treatment includes buprenorphine, venlafaxine and prazosin. Mood and anxiety improved during follow-up, but severe daytime sleepiness persisted despite reducing venlafaxine. He falls asleep unintentionally on public transport and while reading, and reports sometimes sleeping for very prolonged periods.
Further history includes brief hand weakness when laughing, visual/tactile experiences while falling asleep and repeated awakening with inability to move.
