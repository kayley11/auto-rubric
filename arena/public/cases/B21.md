## Decision point

During elective open hernia surgery after two separate cord-like structures are seen, before postoperative ultrasound or follow-up.

## Provisional review points

Distinguish apparent duplicated cord/vas anatomy from other surgical structures and preserve both vascular/ductal pathways while seeking appropriate specialist judgement. Avoid cutting or sacrificing an unidentified structure merely to fit usual anatomy. Document the finding and the limits of visual confirmation; do not infer a postoperative perfusion result. Preoperative non-detection of an unexpected variant is not itself an information-seeking failure in an uncomplicated hernia. Original ultrasound images were not exportable later; that is a record limitation, not a negative study.
