## Model-visible facts and task

A prospective uncontrolled series enrolled 15 people with advanced cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma of the head and neck awaiting surgery. All received neoadjuvant capecitabine.
Ten of 15 had clinical tumour reduction. Twelve underwent surgery; five of those twelve had a complete pathological response and seven of twelve were downstaged. Two people with apparent complete clinical lesion resolution chose radiotherapy instead of surgery; another did not proceed to surgery after progression. No grade 3 or higher treatment-related toxicity was reported. There was no comparator group, and follow-up for durability was short.
