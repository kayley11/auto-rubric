## Answer

**Summary**

A 30-year-old alert adult presents with a mild headache following a missed meal. No injury, focal weakness, fever, or neck stiffness is reported. No examination findings or test results have been provided.

**Possible explanations**

The presentation is most consistent with a benign, non-specific headache, possibly related to hypoglycaemia or dehydration from skipping lunch. Tension-type headache is a common consideration in this age group.

**Uncertainty**

Without examination or investigations, secondary causes (e.g., meningitis, intracranial pathology) cannot be excluded, though the absence of red-flag features makes these less likely.

**Next steps**

Clinical examination including neurological assessment, hydration, and reassessment. Escalate if red flags emerge.
