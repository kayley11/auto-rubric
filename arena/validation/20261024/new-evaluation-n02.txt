MODEL ANSWER
**Review of your night-time episodes**

Your episodes — shouting, purposeless movements, thrashing, and sometimes wetting the bed between roughly 04:00 and 06:00, with no memory afterwards — could be explained by several possibilities: a sleep-related movement disorder (parasomnia), a seizure occurring during sleep, or a metabolic cause such as low blood sugar. Your untreated moderate obstructive sleep apnoea may contribute to disrupted sleep, making some of these episodes more likely.

No glucose measurements appear in your available records. If obtained, they would help exclude or confirm low blood sugar as a trigger, though a single result would not give a final answer.

Your weight loss, reduced energy, and difficulty concentrating need further investigation to determine whether they share a common cause. We would recommend a sleep medicine review and blood tests. If a severe episode recurs — for example, if you are injured or have difficulty breathing — call 999 for an ambulance.

**Two questions for you and your family:**

1. Have you or your family noticed any stiffening, jerking, or eye-rolling during the episodes? This helps distinguish a seizure from a parasomnia, which guide different treatments.

2. Has anyone recorded a video of an episode? A short clip is very helpful for the sleep or neurology team to see exactly what is happening.
