A 52-year-old man with chronic diarrhoea, neuropathy, weight loss, and new heart failure has imaging most consistent with an infiltrative cardiomyopathy, likely amyloidosis, but the aetiology and subtype remain unconfirmed.

**Interpretation**

The echocardiographic and CMR pattern (symmetric LV thickening, relative apical sparing on strain, elevated native T1, ECV approximately 50%, diffuse subendocardial LGE) strongly supports an infiltrative process, most characteristically amyloidosis. The negative monoclonal workup makes AL amyloidosis less probable but does not exclude it. **Needed before final aetiology**

Tissue confirmation with Congo red staining and amyloid typing (mass spectrometry or immunohistochemistry) is required to establish the diagnosis and subtype. If amyloid is confirmed, TTR genetic testing or further imaging clarification distinguishes ATTRwt from ATTRv. Lyso-Gb3 measurement should close the Fabry gap.
