On physical examination, her blood pressure was 138/86 mmHg, heart rate 78 beats/min, and body mass index was 24.3 kg/m2. Neurological examination was unremarkable. Fasting plasma glucose was 2.8 mmol/L with a concurrent serum insulin level of 186.4 μIU/mL and C-peptide of 12.3 ng/mL, both markedly elevated. A 72-hour fasting test was performed, during which hypoglycemic episodes recurred with Whipple's triad confirmed. Contrast-enhanced abdominal CT revealed a 2.1 × 1.8 cm hypervascular mass in the pancreatic body. Subsequent endoscopic ultrasound confirmed a well-circumscribed lesion consistent with an insulinoma. The patient underwent distal pancreatectomy with splenic preservation. Postoperative fasting glucose normalized, and nocturnal behavioral episodes ceased entirely. Histopathological examination confirmed a well-differentiated pancreatic neuroendocrine tumor (insulinoma, grade 1, Ki-67 index <2%). At six-month follow-up, the patient remained euglycemic with no recurrence of nocturnal episodes.
