She was alert, with coherent speech and cooperative during examination. Neurological examination: orientation, memory, calculation, and judgment were normal. Speech was fluent with clear articulation. Cranial nerve examination was unremarkable. Muscle strength was grade 5/5 in all limbs, with normal tone and symmetric deep tendon reflexes. Pathological reflexes were absent. Superficial and deep sensation, as well as coordination, were normal. Cardiopulmonary and abdominal examinations revealed no significant abnormalities. Laboratory Findings: In-hospital nocturnal glucose monitoring revealed frequent hypoglycemic episodes, with levels dropping as low as 1.2–2.0 mmol/L. Concurrent testing demonstrated inappropriately elevated insulin levels: serum C-peptide (fasting) 2.32 ng/mL, insulin (fasting) 9.96 μU/mL, with a significantly elevated insulin-to-glucose ratio, consistent with hyperinsulinemic hypoglycemia. An oral glucose tolerance test (OGTT) showed a fasting glucose of 3.8 mmol/L and a 2-hour postprandial glucose of 14.4 mmol/L, indicating impaired glucose tolerance. Serum electrolytes, renal and hepatic function, thyroid function, cortisol rhythm, ACTH, and tumor markers were all within normal limits. Neurophysiological Examination: Electroencephalography (EEG) performed after admission revealed no definite epileptiform discharges.
