Curative-intent ifosfamide, cisplatin, and etoposide (ICE) was started because of the prolonged remission, biopsy-proven recurrence, and anticipated chemosensitivity. The planned 28-day regimen comprised ifosfamide 900 mg/m2, cisplatin 20 mg/m2, and etoposide 100 mg/m2 on days 1–5. Scheduled oral desmopressin 360 μg/day, hydrocortisone 30 mg/day, and levothyroxine 50 μg/day were continued during the sodium disturbances following endocrinology consultation, which supported maintenance of the established doses and schedules. Before salvage chemotherapy, serum sodium was 139.3 mEq/L, measured serum osmolality was 278 mOsm/kg, serum creatinine was 0.52 mg/dL, estimated glomerular filtration rate was 163.4 mL/min/1.73 m2, spot urinary sodium was 91.4 mEq/L, and urine osmolality was 555 mOsm/kg. Baseline cortisol was reported as 5.5 μg/dL, but sampling time relative to hydrocortisone was unavailable, and the value was not used to infer adrenal reserve. Baseline laboratory results are provided in Online Resource 3. The course of serum sodium and serum osmolality, oral sodium supplementation, treatment modifications, and urinary sodium/osmolality trends during ICE are shown in Fig. 3a, b. After ICE cycle 1, fatigue, anorexia, and headache accompanied grade 4 hypotonic hyponatremia (Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events [CTCAE] version 5.0): serum sodium reached 118.4 mEq/L on cycle 1 day 9, and measured serum osmolality was 236 mOsm/kg.
