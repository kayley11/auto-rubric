She denied alcohol consumption, tobacco use, or use of sedative-hypnotic medications. There was no family history of epilepsy, sleep disorders, or psychiatric illness. On physical examination, her blood pressure was 148/92 mmHg, pulse 78 beats per minute, respiratory rate 18 breaths per minute, and temperature 36.5°C. Body mass index was 27.4 kg/m2. She was alert and oriented with intact cognition. Neurological examination revealed no focal deficits: cranial nerves were intact, muscle strength was 5/5 in all limbs, deep tendon reflexes were symmetric, and pathological reflexes were absent. The remainder of the systemic examination was unremarkable except for a crowded oropharynx. Laboratory investigations revealed a fasting blood glucose of 3.1 mmol/L, with low fasting insulin and C-peptide levels, excluding insulinoma. Glycated hemoglobin was 5.2%. Thyroid function, liver and renal function tests, electrolytes, and complete blood count were within normal limits. An overnight polysomnography study confirmed moderate obstructive sleep apnea with an apnea-hypopnea index of 22.5 events per hour and a lowest oxygen saturation of 78%. A video-electroencephalogram during sleep captured one typical episode arising from non-rapid eye movement sleep, stage N2, without any epileptiform discharge interictally or ictally.
