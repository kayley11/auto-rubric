On physical examination, her blood pressure was 138/86 mmHg, heart rate 72 beats per minute, and body mass index was 24.3 kg/m². Neurological examination revealed no focal deficits; higher cortical functions, including language, memory, and orientation, were intact. Cranial nerve examination was normal, and motor and sensory examinations showed no abnormalities. Deep tendon reflexes were symmetric, and plantar responses were flexor bilaterally.

Polysomnography performed during the admission revealed moderate obstructive sleep apnea with an apnea-hypopnea index of 24 events per hour, with significant desaturations. Notably, continuous monitoring captured episodes of complex motor activity during non-REM sleep stages, accompanied by marked bradycardia and a drop in blood pressure. Simultaneous continuous glucose monitoring documented recurrent episodes of hypoglycemia, with nadir values of 1.8 mmol/L temporally correlated with the behavioral episodes.

Laboratory investigations revealed fasting blood glucose of 2.1 mmol/L, with elevated fasting insulin at 42.3 μIU/mL (reference range 2.6–24.9) and a C-peptide level of 4.8 ng/mL (reference range 1.1–4.4). The proinsulin level was markedly elevated at 186 pmol/L (reference range 5–76). A 72-hour fasting test was performed, during which the patient developed symptomatic hypoglycemia at 36 hours with a blood glucose of 1.9 mmol/L, concurrent insulin of 58.1 μIU/mL, and C-peptide of 5.2 ng/mL. The sulfonylurea screen was negative.

Contrast-enhanced abdominal computed tomography identified a 2.3 cm hypervascular mass in the body of the pancreas. Endoscopic ultrasound confirmed a well-circumscribed lesion with no evidence of local invasion or lymphadenopathy.

The patient was diagnosed with an insulinoma. She underwent a distal pancreatectomy with splenectomy. Postoperative blood glucose levels normalized within 24 hours, and the nocturnal behavioral episodes ceased entirely. At a six-month follow-up, the patient reported no recurrence of nocturnal episodes, stable weight, and improved daytime energy. The final pathology confirmed a well-differentiated neuroendocrine tumor, grade 1, with negative margins.
