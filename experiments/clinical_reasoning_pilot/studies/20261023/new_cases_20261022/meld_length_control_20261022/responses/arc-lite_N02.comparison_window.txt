On physical examination, her blood pressure was 138/86 mmHg, heart rate 72 beats per minute, and body mass index was 24.3 kg/m2. Neurological examination revealed no focal deficits; higher cortical functions, including language, memory, and orientation, were intact. Cranial nerve examination was normal, and motor and sensory examinations showed no abnormalities. Deep tendon reflexes were symmetric, and plantar responses were flexor bilaterally. Polysomnography performed during the admission revealed moderate obstructive sleep apnea with an apnea-hypopnea index of 24 events per hour, with significant desaturations. Notably, continuous monitoring captured episodes of complex motor activity during non-REM sleep stages, accompanied by marked bradycardia and a drop in blood pressure. Simultaneous continuous glucose monitoring documented recurrent episodes of hypoglycemia, with nadir values of 1.8 mmol/L temporally correlated with the behavioral episodes. Laboratory investigations revealed fasting blood glucose of 2.1 mmol/L, with elevated fasting insulin at 42.3 μIU/mL (reference range 2.6–24.9) and a C-peptide level of 4.8 ng/mL (reference range 1.1–4.4).
