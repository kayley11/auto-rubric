A non-contrast computed tomography scan of the head was normal. Given the acute descending flaccid paralysis, areflexia, bulbar dysfunction, and autonomic instability without a toxic or metabolic explanation, botulism was strongly suspected. The patient was intubated for airway protection and respiratory support. Stool and serum samples were sent for botulinum toxin detection by mouse bioassay. He was transferred to a tertiary care center where heptavalent botulism antitoxin was administered.
