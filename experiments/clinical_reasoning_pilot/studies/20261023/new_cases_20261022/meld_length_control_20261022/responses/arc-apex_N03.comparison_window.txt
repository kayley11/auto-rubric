Given the combination of unexplained coma, hypothermia, bradypnea, and flaccid areflexic paralysis, toxic ingestion was strongly suspected despite the negative initial screen. The patient was intubated for airway protection and started on empirical supportive therapy, including active rewarming with warmed intravenous fluids and a forced diuresis protocol. A collateral history obtained from a family member revealed that the patient had recently obtained botulinum toxin and colchicine through an online source for cosmetic purposes, raising concern for iatrogenic botulism.
