Neurological examination showed neck stiffness and a positive Kernig sign. Brain MRI showed no abnormalities. Contrast-enhanced spinal MRI demonstrated disc herniation at L3–L4 and L4–L5, with possible nerve root compression. CSF analysis showed persistent lymphocytic pleocytosis (178 cells/μL; 97.7% lymphocytes) with a normal protein concentration (36.6 mg/dL). Samples were sent to the Laboratory of Neuroimmunology, II Department of Neurology, Institute of Psychiatry and Neurology in Warsaw for autoimmune antibody testing. Neuronal surface antibodies were tested using indirect immunofluorescence assay (IIFT) on transfected EU90 cells at a serum/CSF dilution of 1:10. Antibodies against NMDAR, AMPAR1/2, DPPX, LGI1, CASPR2, and GABABR were not detected. GFAP-IgG was also tested using IIFT on transfected EU90 cells at a dilution of 1:10 and was detected in both CSF and serum. In addition, the patient underwent extensive infectious and immunological investigations, including HIV testing, testing for Borrelia burgdorferi and tick-borne encephalitis, Treponema pallidum serology, amphiphysin antibodies, lymphocyte immunophenotyping, and repeated molecular testing for infectious pathogens (Table 1).
