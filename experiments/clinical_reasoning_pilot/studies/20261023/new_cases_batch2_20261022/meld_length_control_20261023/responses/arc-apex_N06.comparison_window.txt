At 17:00 (t0), UFH was paused and the first 20-mg alteplase infusion began through right lower-limb deep venous access (Figures 1B). Arterial cannulation-site bleeding occurred at 17:20 and was managed with compression. The 17:39 sample showed platelets of 62 × 109/L, fibrinogen of 1.38 g/L, and aPTT of 47.5 s after the transfusions, and these values were reviewed before any decision on further thrombolysis. Because the patient remained hemodynamically unstable with ongoing catecholamine requirements and echocardiographic evidence of right ventricular dysfunction, a second 20-mg alteplase infusion was started at 18:10 and completed over 30 minutes. UFH remained suspended during both infusions and was resumed at 19:45 with a reduced initial bolus, given the recent fibrinolytic exposure and the arterial-line oozing. No intracranial hemorrhage occurred; surveillance imaging on the following day showed a small retroperitoneal hematoma that did not require intervention, and the arterial line site stabilized with sustained local pressure. The clinical response over the subsequent hours was gradual rather than immediate. Norepinephrine requirements decreased progressively from 0.45 to 0.12 μg/kg/min by 06:00 the next morning, and lactate fell from 4.8 to 1.9 mmol/L over the same interval.
