Transthoracic echocardiography demonstrated a thickened interventricular septum (14 mm) and left atrial dilation with preserved ejection fraction of 55% and a sparkling myocardial texture. Cardiac magnetic resonance imaging showed diffuse subendocardial late gadolinium enhancement and abnormal myocardial nulling at 0 ms, consistent with amyloid infiltration. Serum protein electrophoresis and immunofixation revealed a monoclonal IgG-kappa band, and serum free light chain assay showed a markedly elevated kappa free light chain (186 mg/L) with a kappa/lambda ratio of 42.3. Bone marrow biopsy demonstrated 18% clonal plasma cells. Abdominal fat pad aspiration yielded Congo red–positive amorphous deposits with apple-green birefringence under polarised light, and mass spectrometry confirmed AL (lambda) type amyloid. The patient was initiated on daratumumab, cyclophosphamide, and dexamethasone. At 6-month follow-up, his diarrhoea had resolved, blood pressure improved to 112/70 mmHg, NT-proBNP decreased to 142 pg/mL, and echocardiographic wall thickness reduced to 11 mm, indicating a favourable haematological and organ response.
